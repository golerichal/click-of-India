🎭 Maharashtra Culture

Experience the traditions, festivals and art that make Maharashtra unique.

Ganesh Chaturthi

One of Maharashtra's most celebrated festivals, filled with devotion, music, decorations and community celebrations.

Lavani Dance

A vibrant traditional folk dance of Maharashtra, known for expressive movements, rhythm and colourful costumes.

🍛 Famous Maharashtra Food

Discover some of the delicious flavours Maharashtra is famous for.

Vada Pav

A popular Mumbai street food made with a spicy potato fritter served inside a soft pav with chutneys.

Misal Pav

A spicy Maharashtrian dish made with sprouted beans, spicy gravy, farsan and pav.

📍 Famous Maharashtra Destinations

Explore historic landmarks and iconic places across Maharashtra.

Gateway of India

An iconic landmark of Mumbai overlooking the Arabian Sea and one of Maharashtra's most famous attractions.

Raigad Fort

A historic hill fort with great significance in Maharashtra's history and heritage.
