🎭 Kerala Culture

Discover Kerala's traditional art, festivals and cultural heritage.

Kathakali

A famous traditional dance-drama of Kerala, known for its colourful costumes, makeup and expressive performances.

Onam

Kerala's vibrant harvest festival celebrated with traditional celebrations, flowers, music and feasts.

🍛 Famous Kerala Food

Taste the unique flavours and traditional dishes of Kerala.

Kerala Sadya

A traditional vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf, featuring a variety of delicious Kerala dishes.

Appam & Stew

Soft, fluffy appam paired with a delicious coconut-based vegetable or meat stew.

📍 Famous Kerala Destinations

Explore Kerala's peaceful backwaters, hills and natural beauty.

Kerala Backwaters

Peaceful waterways surrounded by lush greenery, best experienced through a traditional houseboat journey.

Munnar

A beautiful hill station famous for tea plantations, misty mountains and refreshing landscapes.
